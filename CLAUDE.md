# Market Research & Campaign Advisor Tool
## For Academic, Skill-Based & Language Learning Courses in Moldova, Georgia, Armenia

### Markets & Capitals
- **Moldova**: Chișinău (Chișinău Language School, Top English School)
- **Georgia**: Tbilisi (Georgian language schools, tech bootcamps, AZTech Training)
- **Armenia**: Yerevan (ICLT, Berlitz, BDG, 42 Yerevan, AUA)

### Course Types to Analyze
- Language learning (English, Armenian, Georgian, Russian, other EU languages)
- Tech/IT skills (bootcamps, programming, development)
- Professional development & vocational training
- Academic enrichment courses

### Pricing Analysis
**Key Metric**: All pricing distilled to **per academic hour** (normalize 45min-1h15min sessions to hourly rate)

Example calculation:
- Course: 3 months, 6 hours/week = 72 total hours
- Total cost: $1,200
- Hourly rate: $1,200 ÷ 72 = **$16.67/hour**

### Market Tiers (Competitive Intelligence)
Three pricing tiers exist in each market:
1. **Budget Tier**: Lowest price point, high volume, community/group focus
2. **Mid Tier**: Standard quality, established institutes, strong reputation
3. **Premium Tier**: Highest price, specialized instructors, premium positioning

### Instructor Compensation Context
- **Software dev/tech instructors**: $25-99/hour (regional market rate)
- **Language instructors**: €500/month baseline in Moldova, ~812k AMD (~$2,000) in Armenia
- **General course instructors**: Typically 30-40% of course revenue per hour taught

### Analysis Goals
Use competitor data to:
1. Identify pricing gaps and market opportunities
2. Understand course structure patterns (duration, frequency, group size)
3. Determine tier positioning strategy
4. Estimate instructor compensation models
5. Find underserved niches (course types, languages, specializations)
6. Draft competitive lesson plans with market-backed positioning

### Output Requirements
All market analysis should include:
- Competitor names, pricing (hourly rate), course structure
- Tier positioning (budget/mid/premium)
- Instructor rate analysis if available
- 2-3 market opportunities identified
- Pricing recommendation with tier strategy
- 3-5 course topics with demand signals
- Sample 5-8 lesson outline with learning objectives
- Data sources cited for all claims

### Notes
- Data gathered from major platforms and public institute websites (Feb 2026)
- Pricing in local currencies (MDL, GEL, AMD) converted to EUR/USD for comparison
- Instructor rates based on market salary data and contractor rates
- Focus on capital cities only
