Monday/Wednesday or Tuesday/Thursday, 2h15 per session. Times: 9:00 / 11:00 / 16:30 / 18:30. Summer intensive also available (Mon/Wed/Fri, 2 months). Do I need to take a placement test? Yes — unless you have zero English. The test has two parts: a written grammar test (~30–40 min) followed by an oral test at our premises. Call 079 222 447 to schedule. How many students per group? Maximum 8–10 students per group. Small groups are essential to our teaching philosophy — more speaking time, more feedback, faster progress. Can I have individual lessons? Yes. Individual lessons are 60 minutes and cost 500 MDL. We recommend a set of 10 sessions (5000 MDL). Schedule is agreed with your teacher. Available online and offline. Can I attend a free trial lesson? Yes — anyone can attend a trial lesson free of charge. Register in advance to secure your place. Call 079 222 447 or use the registration form. What are the school's opening hours?
