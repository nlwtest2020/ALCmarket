# Lesson Plan Generator Skill

Draft competitive lesson plans backed by market research for new courses.

## Usage Examples

`/lesson-plan-generator python-basics moldova 40-hours beginner`
`/lesson-plan-generator english-professional georgia 60-hours intermediate`
`/lesson-plan-generator digital-marketing armenia 30-hours all-levels`

## Process

1. **Validate market opportunity**:
   - Check competitor lesson structure patterns in market
   - Identify underserved specializations
   - Extract successful course formats from competitors

2. **Structure lesson plan**:
   - **Duration**: Distill to target hour count
   - **Frequency**: Based on market patterns (e.g., 6 hrs/week over 3 months)
   - **Group size**: Aligned with competitive offerings
   - **Format**: Online, hybrid, or in-person based on market

3. **Define content**:
   - **Learning objectives**: Clear, measurable outcomes
   - **Lessons**: 5-8 modules typical for target course type
   - **Each lesson**: Title, hours, key topics, activities, assessment

4. **Incorporate market signals**:
   - Include topics mentioned in competitor courses
   - Align with demonstrated student demand
   - Match instructor compensation models

5. **Add competitive differentiation**:
   - Identify gaps in competitor offerings
   - Suggest specializations or combinations not offered
   - Position unique value prop relative to market

6. **Draft instructor notes**:
   - Time allocation per lesson
   - Resource requirements
   - Assessment rubrics
   - Suggested student-to-instructor ratios
