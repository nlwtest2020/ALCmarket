# Campaign Planner Skill

Generate comprehensive launch campaign strategies for new courses based on market research.

## Usage Examples

`/campaign-planner python-course moldova launch-strategy`
`/campaign-planner english-professionals georgia positioning market-fit`
`/campaign-planner tech-skills-bundle armenia channel-strategy`

## Campaign Planning Framework

1. **Market Positioning**:
   - Analyze competitor positioning in target market
   - Identify gaps and differentiation opportunities
   - Define unique value proposition vs. competition
   - Position in tier (budget/mid/premium) with rationale

2. **Target Audience Analysis**:
   - Extract student profiles from competitor offerings
   - Identify underserved segments
   - Analyze demand signals (job market, regional trends)
   - Define ideal student persona

3. **Pricing Strategy**:
   - Load pricing analysis from market data
   - Recommend tier-based pricing
   - Suggest promotional pricing for launch
   - Define payment options

4. **Go-to-Market Channels**:
   - Map effective channels in market (social media, partnerships, local platforms)
   - Identify key influencers or partnerships
   - Recommend content strategy
   - Timing and launch phases

5. **Messaging & Content**:
   - Define key messaging (outcomes, ROI, career advancement)
   - Create messaging variants for each segment
   - Suggest content pillars
   - Recommend testimonial/case study sources

6. **Instructor Strategy**:
   - Recommend instructor hiring/compensation model
   - Suggest qualifications and backgrounds
   - Plan instructor onboarding and support
   - Define instructor incentive structure

7. **Launch Plan & Timeline**:
   - Pre-launch phase (audience building, beta testing)
   - Launch phase (promotion, first cohort)
   - Post-launch (community building, iteration)
   - Success metrics and KPIs

## Output Structure

**Campaign Plan for [Course] in [Market]**

1. Market Opportunity Summary
2. Competitive Positioning
3. Target Audience Profile
4. Pricing Strategy & Revenue Model
5. Go-to-Market Channels
6. Messaging Framework
7. Instructor Strategy
8. 90-Day Launch Timeline
9. Success Metrics & KPIs
10. Risk Mitigation
