# Market Research Summary
## Moldova, Georgia, Armenia - February 2026

### Executive Summary

This document summarizes competitive intelligence for launching educational courses (language, tech, professional development) in the capital cities of Moldova, Georgia, and Armenia. Data was gathered from public institute websites, course platforms, and market salary sources.

---

## Moldova (Chișinău)

### Market Overview
- **Population**: ~3.7M nationally, Chișinău ~700k
- **Internet penetration**: High in capital
- **Primary demand**: Language learning (Russian, English, Romanian), some tech training
- **Market maturity**: Established language school sector, limited tech bootcamps

### Competitors Found

| Name | Type | Tier | Hourly Rate (EUR) | Notes |
|------|------|------|------------------|-------|
| Chisinau Language School | Language | Mid | €30 | Individual lessons |
| Top English School | Language | Budget | €3.33 | Group courses, 96 hrs |
| DGNI | Language | Mid | €15 | Romanian, professional |

### Pricing Tiers
- **Budget**: €3/hour (group language courses)
- **Mid**: €15/hour (established schools, quality reputation)
- **Premium**: €30/hour (individual/specialized instruction)

### Instructor Market
- **Teacher salary**: €500/month average
- **Hourly equivalent**: ~€3/hour
- **Market growth**: IT sector showing significant salary increases (2025)

### Key Opportunities
1. **Tech skills gap**: Minimal tech bootcamp competition, high demand
2. **Mid-tier positioning**: Few options between €3 and €30/hour
3. **Specialized combinations**: Language + tech courses rare
4. **Professional development**: Limited niche courses for career advancement

### Market Gaps
- Premium tech bootcamp offerings
- Group-based skill training (vs. individual lessons)
- Professional development for non-tech sectors
- Specialized language combinations (e.g., English + business)

## Georgia (Tbilisi)

### Market Overview
- **Population**: ~3.7M nationally, Tbilisi ~1.2M
- **Internet penetration**: Very high, tech-forward population
- **Primary demand**: Language learning, tech bootcamps, professional skills
- **Market maturity**: Highly developed, competitive, diverse offerings

### Competitors Found

| Name | Type | Tier | Hourly Rate (EUR) | Notes |
|------|------|------|------------------|-------|
| Georgian Courses | Language | Mid | €15 | 1-to-1, 20 hrs/week |
| Language International | Language | Budget | ~€12 | Group courses, starting price |
| The Knowledge Academy | Tech | Premium | €100 | Bootcamp intensive |
| AZTech Training | Tech | Premium | ~€100 | Bootcamp, international standards |

### Pricing Tiers
- **Budget**: €12/hour (group language courses, high volume)
- **Mid**: €15/hour (one-to-one language instruction)
- **Premium**: €100/hour (tech bootcamps, intensive programs)

### Instructor Market
- **Software dev rates**: $25-99/hour (marketplace data)
- **Remote work**: Significant growth in remote/hybrid arrangements
- **Trend**: Growing demand for tech talent, salary pressure upward

### Key Opportunities
1. **Mid-tier tech gap**: Between €15/hour basic courses and €100/hour bootcamps
2. **Specialized niche**: Tech + language combinations (e.g., Georgian + coding)
3. **Professional development**: Skills training for non-IT sectors
4. **Group tech courses**: Bootcamp pricing but smaller commitment

### Market Gaps
- Mid-tier tech skills training (e.g., €30-50/hour)
- Professional language training (business, specialized)
- Hybrid tech + soft skills courses
- Executive/professional development programs

### Market Gaps
- Premium-tier language training (besides Berlitz)
- Specialized professional development (non-tech)
- Tech + soft skills combinations
- Executive/career advancement programs

## Cross-Market Comparison

### Pricing Bands
```
BUDGET TIER:        Moldova €3  | Georgia €12  | Armenia $8
MID TIER:           Moldova €15 | Georgia €15  | Armenia $12
PREMIUM TIER:       Moldova €30 | Georgia €100 | Armenia $25
```

**Key insight**: Georgia's premium tier is 3-4x higher due to tech bootcamp pricing.

### Instructor Compensation
- **Moldova**: ~€3/hour (based on €500/mo salary)
- **Georgia**: $25-99/hour (software dev market rate)
- **Armenia**: ~$12/hour (based on ~$2k/mo salary)

**Implication**: Instructor margins vary significantly; Georgia tech requires premium pricing to sustain.

### Market Characteristics

| Factor | Moldova | Georgia | Armenia |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| Tech bootcamp strength | Weak | Strong | Moderate |
| Language school maturity | High | Very high | High |
| Free/subsidized options | None noted | None noted | Significant |
| Premium positioning | Limited | Strong | Limited |
| Diaspora factor | Low | Low | High |
| Growth rate | Moderate | High | Moderate |

## Strategic Recommendations

### If targeting budget tier:
- **Best market**: Armenia (abundant free/subsidized competitors, price-sensitive)
- **Strategy**: Differentiate on quality, not price
- **Hourly rate**: €3-8/hour in Moldova/Georgia; $8-12 in Armenia

### If targeting mid tier:
- **Best market**: All three are viable
- **Strategy**: Position as "quality with value" - better than budget, cheaper than premium
- **Hourly rate**: €15/hour Moldova/Georgia; $12/hour Armenia

### If targeting premium tier:
- **Best market**: Georgia (€100 bootcamp pricing established)
- **Strategy**: Specialized, brand-driven (like Berlitz), or intensive bootcamp model
- **Hourly rate**: €30/hour Moldova; €100 Georgia (tech); $25 Armenia

### Underserved Opportunities
1. **Mid-tier tech in Georgia**: $30-50/hour courses (gap between €15 and €100)
2. **Premium language in Armenia**: $25-35/hour (only Berlitz at $25)
3. **Hybrid courses**: Tech + language or tech + business skills (all markets)
4. **Diaspora-focused**: Armenian language targeted at diaspora (global market)
5. **Professional development**: Career advancement courses for non-tech sectors (all markets)

## Data Quality Notes

**Sources**: Public institute websites, Language International, course platform aggregators, market salary data (TimeCamp, Paylab, Trading Economics)

**Limitations**:
- Pricing may be outdated (data from web search results)
- Not all institutes publicly list pricing; some estimated
- Local market may have additional competitors not online
- Seasonal pricing variations not captured
- Corporate/bulk pricing not included

**Recommendations for deeper research**:
- Contact institutes directly for current 2026 pricing
- Survey students on willingness-to-pay
- Analyze competitor student reviews and satisfaction

- Investigate instructor recruitment challenges
